Wallpaper samples

Ten seamless tiles for the cream ground, answering “a bit too light, I can’t see it.” Every band below is a real 380 px phone width with the tile set as an actual repeating background and opaque stopover cards on top — so you are judging the pattern the way you will meet it, under an itinerary rather than beside one.

Ordered lightest → boldest by mean luminance drop across the tile — the number that tracks “can I see it from arm’s length”. The | tick on each bar marks today’s wallpaper. ink % is how much of the tile carries any mark at all; peak ΔL is how dark the darkest mark gets — a tile can be sparse yet bold (few marks, strong ones) or dense yet quiet (many marks, faint ones).
